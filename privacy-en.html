How information is handled

The app has no account registration, ads, third-party analytics, or tracking SDK. This release has no developer-operated data server. Photos and reminders are not uploaded to the developer. System services described below may use the network.

Photos and location

You select photos using Apple’s system picker. Processing, watermarks, grids, and text recognition run on your device. Live Photo processing creates temporary files in the app sandbox, which are removed after processing, preview, and saving release them. The system picker may download photos you store in iCloud.

The app reads existing camera settings and, when available, GPS metadata. New installations disable original metadata retention and exact coordinate display by default. You can enable either option. Removing file metadata does not remove text already drawn on the image.

Optional city lookup sends the photo’s existing coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service. It does not request your device’s current location. Apple’s privacy policy applies to its services: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Saving supports Live Photos. The sharing button sends a still preview.

Reminders and wallpapers

With permission, the app reads, creates, edits, completes, and deletes Apple Reminders. Your system iCloud settings may sync these changes to other devices. We do not operate a separate reminders server.

A selected wallpaper background is resized and stored in an App Group container, shared with the app’s shortcut actions. Delete it in To-do Wallpaper > Background > Delete Saved Background Photo. Copies in Photos and the system wallpaper must be removed separately. Wallpaper images are snapshots and require running Refresh after tasks change.

Settings, actions, and permissions

Launcher items, favorites, wallpaper preferences, watermark preferences, and entitlement cache are stored locally. Widgets share relevant settings through App Groups. Motion data is used only when you run its device actions. Biometric authentication is handled by the system; the app does not receive biometric templates.

Links and shortcuts you choose can open other apps or sites, which follow their own policies. All shortcuts in the library are made by us, signed, and bundled with the app; adding one hands the file to Apple’s Shortcuts app without downloading anything. The catalog is updated with app releases, not downloaded from our servers.

Reminders permission enables task management. Add Photos permission enables saving. Motion and biometric permissions apply only to the corresponding actions. Permissions can be revoked in iOS Settings. Refusing Reminders access does not prevent use of photo tools.

Purchases and contact

Apple processes Pro purchases and payments. The app verifies and caches entitlements without obtaining bank or card information. Restoring purchases contacts the App Store. Existing valid entitlements remain recognized. Request refunds through https://reportaproblem.apple.com/.

Contact the operator using the email above for privacy questions and rights requests. Policy changes will update this document and its date.
